## Target audience & content
- **Target age groups:** Recommended **13+** to keep submission simple. The app is genuinely
  all‑ages‑appropriate (no ads, no data collection, no purchases), so you *may* include
  younger brackets if you want the broader audience — but selecting an under‑13 group opts you
  into Google Play's **Families policy** requirements and extra review. The app already meets
  those requirements substantively; choosing 13+ simply avoids the extra process.
- **Appeal to children:** The retro/cartoon style may appeal to children. Answer honestly; if
  you target 13+, indicate the app is not specifically directed at children under 13.
- **Store presence for families:** Only opt into "Designed for Families / Teacher Approved" if
  you deliberately target younger ages.
