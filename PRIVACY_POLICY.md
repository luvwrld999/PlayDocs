# Privacy Policy — Retro Rumble

*Last updated: July 21, 2026. Host this at a public URL and link it in the Play Console
under App content → Privacy policy.*

## Summary

Retro Rumble is a single-player and local-multiplayer retro game collection. The developer
does **not** collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no
analytics, and no servers. Everything the game saves — your scores, coins, unlocks, avatar,
and settings — stays on your device.

## Information We Collect

**None.** The developer does not collect personal information of any kind. The app does not
request your email, contacts, photos, precise location, or advertising identifier, and it
contains no third‑party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

## Information Stored On Your Device

To make the game work, the following is saved locally on your device only (using Android's
standard app storage) and is never transmitted to us:

- High scores, coins, and unlocked shells / screens / button themes
- Achievements, mastery ranks, quests, seasons, and run history
- Your avatar, profile icon, and a display name you may optionally set (defaults to "PLAYER")
- Your settings (audio, accessibility, controller, selected console theme)

This data is removed if you uninstall the app or clear its storage. You can also export or
import your save as a file from the in‑game Settings.

## Local Multiplayer (Bluetooth Versus)

Retro Rumble includes an optional local head‑to‑head mode that connects two nearby devices
directly over Bluetooth / Wi‑Fi Direct using Google's Nearby Connections. When you choose to
host or join a match, your chosen display name is shared **directly with the other player's
device** so they can find and connect to you. No data is sent to the developer or any server,
and nothing is stored off your device. This happens only while you are actively using the
versus feature.

## Permissions

The app requests only what the local versus feature needs:

- **Bluetooth** (advertise / connect / scan) and **Nearby Wi‑Fi Devices** — to discover and
  connect to a nearby player. The scan permission is declared `neverForLocation`.
- **Location** (legacy, older Android only) — required by the Android system for Bluetooth
  device scanning on older versions. It is **not** used to determine or collect your
  geographic location.

The app also uses vibration (haptic feedback) for button presses.

## Children's Privacy

The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect data from anyone, including children
under 13.

## Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties because we do not collect any.
