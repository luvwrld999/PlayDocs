# Play Console — Content Rating (IARC) Answers (Retro Rumble)

Complete Play Console → App content → Content ratings. Category: **Game**. Answer the IARC
questionnaire honestly as below. Expected outcome: **Everyone / PEGI 3–7**.

## Violence
- Cartoon / fantasy violence only: yes, **mild**. Abstract pixel shooting and dodging
  (invaders, birds, asteroids, waves) and spooky‑themed "banishing" in the horror‑archetype
  cartridges. **No blood, no gore, no realistic violence.**
- Realistic violence / blood / gore → **No.**

## Fear / horror
- A few cartridges use atmospheric spooky themes (ghosts, bats, candles, static, sigils) with
  original, non‑infringing art. Intensity is **mild / cartoonish**, not graphic horror.

## Sexual content, nudity, crude humor → **No.**

## Language → **No.**

## Controlled substances (drugs, alcohol, tobacco) → **No.**

## Gambling
- Real‑money gambling or simulated gambling → **No.**
- The in‑game "capsule" is a cosmetic reward bought with **coins earned by playing** — no
  real money and no real‑world value. If asked about randomized virtual items, disclose that
  it grants cosmetics only, purchasable with earned in‑game currency.

## Purchases
- Does the app offer digital purchases (real money) → **No.** (Free, no IAP.)

## User interaction
- **Users can interact:** **Yes** — local Bluetooth versus lets two nearby players play
  head‑to‑head.
- Does the app share the user's current physical location with other users → **No.**
- Can users communicate (chat / user‑generated content) → **No** (gameplay only; no chat, no
  UGC exchange).

## Miscellaneous
- Digital purchases → No. Unrestricted internet access → No. This is not a
  browser/social/dating app.
